Blackjack

Guide:
1. Click Start
2. Type Bet Amount (between 30 and 100)
3. Click one of the following:

3a. Hit: Player gains card
3b. Stand: Give turn to dealer, he hits until 17 or higher
3c. Double Down: Double your bet, hit, then stand.
3d. Surrender: Give up half your bet, dealer wins

4. Once round ends click start again to start a new round
For more information, visit the Blackjack Wikipedia Article.
