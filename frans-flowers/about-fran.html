About Fran

The heart behind Fran's Flowers

Meet Francine Miller

Francine Miller, known to friends and customers as Fran, opened Fran's Flowers in 1995 with a simple goal: to help people share kindness through flowers.

What began as a small neighborhood flower stand has grown into a beloved Mission District shop known for colorful bouquets, friendly service, and thoughtful arrangements for every occasion.

Fran believes that flowers can brighten a room, comfort a friend, celebrate a milestone, and bring people together. Her favorite arrangements use seasonal blooms, bold colors, and a personal touch.

Fran's Mission

Fran's Flowers is rooted in community. Every bouquet is made with care, and ten percent of each sale supports local efforts to fight homelessness across the Bay Area.

For Fran, flowers are more than decorations. They are a way to spread beauty, generosity, and connection throughout San Francisco.
